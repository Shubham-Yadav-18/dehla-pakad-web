The Objective

Dehla Pakad (Catch the Tens) is a strategic 4-player, 2-team partnership game played with a standard 52-card deck. Your team's ultimate goal is to capture the four 10s (Dehlas) from the deck by winning tricks. The team that captures all four Dehlas in a single hand scores a massive victory known as a "Kot."

Phase 1: The Deal & Discovering Trump

Our automated dealer handles the complex setup perfectly:

  1. First Deal: Every player receives exactly 5 cards to begin the round.
  2. Discovering Trump: The game begins immediately without a known trump suit. Players must follow the lead suit. The very first time a player is unable to follow the lead suit, the suit they play becomes the Trump Suit for the rest of the hand.
  3. Second Deal: Once the Trump suit is discovered, the dealer automatically distributes the remaining 8 cards to each player, completing the 13-card hand.

Phase 2: Winning Tricks & Sweeping

Winning a single trick does not mean you immediately collect the cards. Cards accumulate in a pile in the center of the table until a player executes a Sweep. To sweep the accumulated pile and secure the Dehlas inside it for your team, you must achieve one of the following:

  • Consecutive Wins: A single player (not just the team) must win two consecutive tricks to sweep the table.
  • The 13th Trick: The team that wins the final (13th) trick of the round automatically sweeps whatever cards remain on the table.

Phase 3: Scoring & The "Kot"

At the end of the round, teams count the number of 10s (Dehlas) they have swept from the table:

  • If Team A captures 3 Dehlas and Team B captures 1 Dehla, Team A wins the hand.
  • If Team A captures all 4 Dehlas, they score a Kot, which is the ultimate victory and awards maximum points for that round.

Sudden-Death Bowni

To increase the intensity of the game, our platform features a high-stakes Bowni Declaration Phase. After the second deal, a 10-second timer begins.

During this window, any team can call a "Bowni." By doing so, that team publicly wagers that they will capture all 4 Dehlas (a Kot). If they succeed, their points are multiplied. If the opposing team captures even a single Dehla, the round instantly ends in a Sudden-Death victory for the defenders!
