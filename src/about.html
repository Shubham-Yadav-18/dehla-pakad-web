A Cultural Legacy

Dehla Pakad (Hindi for "Catch the Tens") is more than just a card game; it is a beloved piece of cultural heritage. For generations, it has been the centerpiece of family gatherings, festivals, and late-night strategy sessions across South Asia. Our mission is to preserve the authentic spirit of this classic game while bringing it into the modern digital era.

The Digital Experience

We built this platform by combining deep respect for traditional rules with cutting-edge Real-Time WebSocket Technology. Instead of playing against predictable AI, our architecture is engineered exclusively for genuine, 4-player multiplayer experiences. Whether you are across the room or across the globe, our servers ensure that every card thrown, every "Kot" called, and every sudden-death "Bowni" phase happens instantly, with zero lag.

Commitment to Fair Play

Trust and competitive integrity are the foundations of our platform. We have engineered a strict server-authoritative logic system that mathematically validates every single move. Our environment is entirely free of bots, manipulated RNG, or pay-to-win mechanics. We provide the table; pure strategy and teamwork determine the winner.

Because we value your privacy, our game operates on a highly secure, transient-memory architecture. Game states exist only while the match is active and are permanently dissolved the moment the room closes, ensuring your session remains entirely yours.
