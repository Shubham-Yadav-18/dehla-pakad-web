How to Play

🃏
1. The Deal

Everyone gets 5 cards. The game starts without a Trump suit.

♠️
2. Discovering Trump

You must follow suit. The first time a player breaks suit, that new suit permanently becomes Trump!

🔥
3. The Bowni Bet

After all 13 cards are dealt, call Bowni to bet you'll catch ALL FOUR 10s. If the enemy catches even one, you lose instantly.

⚔️
4. Playing the Tricks

The player who discovered Trump leads first. You must follow suit. If you can't, you must play Trump—unless your partner is currently winning the trick, then you can play anything!

🧹
5. Sweeping the Pile

Winning a trick leaves the cards on the table. The same player must win TWO tricks in a row to capture the pile.

🏆
6. 13th Trick & Scoring

The winner of the final trick sweeps everything left on the table. Most 10s wins the round!
